Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about team sizing, stay, pass codes, and judging

What is the unique Pass Code (HS-PASS-XXXX) and how does it work?

➜

Upon completing registration, your team is instantly issued a unique cryptographic code (e.g. HS-PASS-9821). This code gives your leader a 72-hour window to modify team roster details (member names, emails, contact info, domain track). On Day 1 of the hackathon (Jan 28, 2027), you will also input this code in the Theme Vault to decrypt the live problem statements!

Is there any registration fee to participate?

➜

Zero! Participation in Udbhav 2027 is completely free for all shortlisted teams. Accommodation, food, high-speed internet, and mentor support are fully provided at Poornima Institute, Jaipur.

Who is eligible to participate?

➜

Any current undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma student enrolled in an accredited Indian institution is eligible. Cross-college teams and interdisciplinary squads (designers + coders) are highly welcomed!

What are the accommodation and food arrangements?

➜

PIET provides secure, separate resting zones and dormitories for male and female participants with 24/7 campus security. 4 hot meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, midnight snacks) plus unlimited chai/coffee will be served throughout the 36 hours.

How is our registration data stored and protected?

➜

All squad records are stored securely in a private, encrypted database accessible strictly by the authorized Organizing Committee. Team leaders can review and update their roster details within 72 hours using their confidential Pass Code.

How is overnight security and accommodation managed for female participants?
