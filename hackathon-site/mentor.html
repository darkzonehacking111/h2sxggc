OFFICIAL MENTOR CODE OF CONDUCT & EVALUATION PROTOCOL

7 Cardinal Principles of Jury Governance • Multi-Round Compliance • Real-time Audit Trail

RULE 01

Mandatory 3-Round Evaluation Cadence

Every assigned squad must be formally reviewed across Round 1 (Ideation & Feasibility), Round 2 (Architecture & Prototype), and Round 3 (Finale Production & Demo). Skipping rounds or scoring only at finale is strictly forbidden.

RULE 02

5 Standardized Parameters (100 Pts / Round)

Each round must be scored on exactly 5 parameters out of 20 points each: (1) Innovation & Novelty, (2) Technical Architecture, (3) Practical Feasibility, (4) UI/UX Craft, and (5) Live Demo & Defense. Maximum round score is 100 points.

RULE 03

Direct Git Commit Verification

No score may be awarded solely on slide presentations or Figma designs. Mentors must inspect the team's live repository, verify commit cadence during the 36-hour sprint, and confirm that core business logic was authored at Udbhav 2027.

RULE 04

Strict Anti-Plagiarism & Fresh Code Policy

Pre-written codebases and uncredited boilerplate are grounds for immediate disqualification. Open-source libraries and APIs are permitted, but architectural glue, custom algorithms, and system integrations must be fresh sprint commits.

RULE 05

Conflict of Interest & Mandatory Recusal

Mentors must immediately recuse themselves from evaluating squads where personal, familial, collegiate lab, or direct institutional affiliations exist. The Master Admin will reassign alternate jury evaluators.

RULE 06

Constructive Engineering Feedback Mandate

Every evaluated scorecard must contain at least one specific technical commendation and one actionable engineering critique to guide the squad toward production viability.

RULE 07

Cryptographic Timestamping & Score Lockdown

Locked scores are cryptographically timestamped and transmitted directly to the Master Admin console and live standings. Once locked, adjustments require Master Admin authorization to ensure zero tampering or scoring embargo leaks.

ROUND 2
Architecture & Prototype

Midway check: Functional core engine, modular code, API/smart contract wiring & test harness.

→
ROUND 3
Finale Production & Demo

Final defense: End-to-end working system, zero-crash live demo, UI/UX polish & jury defense.

01

1. Innovation & Conceptual Novelty (0–20 pts)

Originality of core concept, non-trivial approach, uniqueness beyond stock boilerplate templates, creative multi-agent, cryptographic, or algorithmic architecture.

5 pts: Derivative / Boilerplate 10 pts: Good Practical Twist 15 pts: Strong Unique Pivot 20 pts: Breakthrough Innovation
02

2. Technical Architecture & Code Quality (0–20 pts)

Repository cleanliness, commit cadence, modular system architecture, robust error boundaries, API/smart-contract security, test suite, and performance benchmarks.

5 pts: Unstructured / Fragile 10 pts: Functional Modules 15 pts: Clean & Resilient 20 pts: Production-Grade Excellence
03

3. Practical Feasibility & Scalability (0–20 pts)

Real-world viability, economic rationale, addressable market impact, infrastructure efficiency, latency bounds, operational maintainability, and cloud/edge scalability.

5 pts: Theoretical Only 10 pts: Actionable MVP 15 pts: High Scalability 20 pts: Enterprise Ready
04

4. UI/UX Craft & User Experience (0–20 pts)

Visual elegance, intuitive user flow, accessibility, state responsiveness, error feedback, micro-interactions, and clear data visualization.

5 pts: Rudimentary UI 10 pts: Clean Layout 15 pts: Polished UX Flow 20 pts: World-Class Aesthetic
05

5. Working Prototype, Polish & Jury Defense (0–20 pts)

Live working demonstration with zero runtime crashes, handling of realistic edge cases, articulate technical pitch, and depth of technical defense during mentor Q&A.

5 pts: Mockups / Crashes 10 pts: Partial Working Demo 15 pts: Flawless Live Demo 20 pts: Exemplary Demo & Defense
